Meet William

A Life Spent Getting the Details Right

William Chesser has spent his career across three worlds that don't usually mix: running businesses, serving in Fayetteville city government, and teaching in public schools. Each one required the same thing - paying close attention to details other people miss.

As a business owner, he's run Peak Furniture for 19 years and The Castle Foundry for 26, holding 2 U.S. and 7 international patents along the way for modular construction and furniture assembly systems. Earlier in his career, he was also a co-founder and COO of a land planning firm, where he helped consult on a multi-million dollar land planning project.

Chesser also served six years as a City of Fayetteville Planning Commissioner and has volunteered on the city's Board of Adjustment and Planning Commission since 2008. He's spent 18 years working in Northwest Arkansas public schools - 16 as a teacher, including 7 years in Springdale - and holds a B.A. in Philosophy and a B.A. in Anthropology from the University of Arkansas, along with a Master's in Anthropology. His philosophy thesis focused on ethics and moral responsibility.

Voters deserve a choice. No candidate should run unopposed. I am running because Washington County's Treasurer should answer to the voters and not to insiders trying to line their pockets. William Chesser

Why This Race Matters

Why William Is Running

William got into this race because he doesn't believe any county office should go unanswered, and because he believes Washington County deserves a Treasurer who answers to voters, not to whoever's already inside the courthouse.

His opponent has never had a job outside the county offices his own family runs. William doesn't think an office responsible for $150 million in taxpayer money should be handed down like an inheritance.
